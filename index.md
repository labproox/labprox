# Lab ProX

Lab ProX is Windows software for a medical laboratory. Staff use it to register patients, record tests and results, print bills and PDF reports, and keep a history of the work.

Patient records stay on the laboratory computer. Lab ProX does not run a cloud database of those records.

## What a laboratory can do

- Register patients and keep a medical-record number, phone number, and national identity number when the laboratory has one
- Enter test definitions, reference ranges, panels, and referring doctors
- Record results, including results received from a connected analyzer
- Print bills and laboratory reports as PDFs
- Give each staff member their own sign-in, with a role
- Keep the database on that PC, including after the program is updated

## If the computer is lost

The laboratory can connect its own Google account from Settings. Lab ProX then uploads a copy of the database to a folder named **LabProX Backups** in that account, about once an hour. The laboratory can download the newest copy onto a new PC and restore it.

Connecting Google Drive is optional. Until someone connects it, the only copy is the one on the laboratory computer.

## License

The program starts with a 30-day trial on that computer. After the trial, the laboratory needs a license key from Lab ProX. The key is checked inside the program. Lab ProX does not contact a website to decide whether the key is valid.
